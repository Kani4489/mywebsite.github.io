Who We Are

Terranex Technologies Private Limited is a specialized engineering and technology company focused on delivering advanced solutions across mission-critical domains.

Our team comprises experienced professionals, technologists, and defense industry veterans who collectively bring more than five decades of practical experience. We combine deep domain knowledge with emerging technologies to create solutions that enhance operational efficiency, improve decision-making, and support national technological self-reliance.

To become a globally recognized technology leader in Geospatial Intelligence, Positioning Systems, Defense Electronics, and Critical Infrastructure Solutions by delivering innovative, reliable, and indigenous technologies that contribute to national development, security, and sustainable growth.

  • To design and deliver world-class geospatial and defense technology solutions.
  • To enable customers with accurate, reliable, and actionable intelligence.
  • To support national self-reliance through indigenous technology development.
  • To foster innovation through continuous research and engineering excellence.
  • To build long-term partnerships based on trust, quality, and customer success.
  • To create sustainable value for stakeholders through technology leadership and operational excellence.
